About Us

SQKO builds solar DC power meters that bring clean, reliable electricity to homes and small businesses. Every meter you buy connects to this platform, so you can recharge it in seconds — prepaid or postpaid, weekly or monthly — without waiting on a queue or a paper token.

Once your meter is registered, your recharges, tokens and history all live here in one place, and our team is on hand through WhatsApp and email whenever you need us.
