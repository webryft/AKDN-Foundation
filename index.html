Why we don't promise daily interest

We'd rather be honest than exciting

A lot of "savings" platforms promise your balance will grow automatically every day, no matter what. We don't do that — here's why, plainly.

No guaranteed daily returnsReal money doesn't grow on its own just for sitting in an account. Any platform promising a fixed daily percentage is paying old users with new users' deposits — that always collapses eventually.
Rewards are earned, not promisedYou're only credited a referral bonus once a real referral is verified — not on a timer, not based on your balance size.
Withdrawals are reviewed by peopleEvery withdrawal is checked by our team before payout. It may take a little longer than an instant auto-approval — but it means your money is actually there.
Questions

Good to know

Does my saved balance earn interest automatically? ▾

No. Your savings balance only changes when you add money, when a verified referral reward is credited, or when a withdrawal is processed. We don't apply any automatic daily growth.

How do referral rewards work? ▾

You get a personal referral code when you sign up. When someone creates and verifies an account using your code, you receive a fixed reward credited to your balance — not a percentage of anything.

How long do withdrawals take? ▾

Withdrawal requests are reviewed manually by our team before payout. You'll see the status change from "Pending" to "Approved" on your dashboard once it's processed.
